ThinkPad T440 Hardware Profile
===============================

CPU: Intel Core i5-4300U
- 2 cores, 4 threads
- 1.9 GHz base, 2.9 GHz turbo
- Haswell microarchitecture
- Supports: SSE4.2, AVX2, BMI2, VMX

Chipset: Intel 8 Series (Lynx Point)
- PCIe 2.0
- SATA 3.0 (6 Gbps)
- USB 3.0
- Intel HD Audio

Keyboard: PS/2 (internal)
- Standard 8042 controller
- IRQ 1
- Scancode Set 2

Display: Intel HD Graphics 4400
- VESA-compatible framebuffer
- Up to 1600×900 (T440 panel)

Disk: SATA
- AHCI controller
- Standard SATA 3.0

Ethernet: Intel i218-LM
- PCIe
- Standard Intel Ethernet

Audio: Intel HDA
- Standard HDA controller
